Our method

How we verify

Every number we post can be traced back to where it came from.

Where the numbers come from

Licensed football data services (football-data.org), collected automatically. We never copy numbers from other websites or accounts. Every page shows when its data was collected.

Results wait to settle

A final score is only used once it has stayed the same for 10 minutes and been seen in at least 2 separate checks. Until then the match shows "Result being checked".

Every number is checked again before posting

Each post is written from a stored record. Just before it goes out, every number and name in it is matched against that record again, and anything that doesn't match blocks the post. Posts are only made from data collected in the last 20 minutes.

Stats and stories are worked out, never typed in

Team pages, leaderboards, extra tables and matchday stories are calculated from the stored results. Our own tables are ordered by points, goal difference and goals scored; leagues add tie-breakers of their own, so the official table stays the reference for positions.

Comparisons need the full picture

Words like "longest", "biggest" or "no other side" only appear when every team or match in that competition has been checked.

One or two sources

Right now our stats come from one source, and each stat page says so. When a second, independent source is added, scores and tables will need both to agree before anything is posted.

Corrections

If a source changes a result after we have used it, our system flags it straight away so the record can be corrected.

What we don't do

No transfer rumours, no injury news, no betting tips and no invented numbers.
